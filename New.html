From a Library to a Launchpad for Young Minds.

NALC — New Age Learning Centre — was born from a simple question: What if a library could be a place young people actually love to be?

In Miao, Arunachal Pradesh, an existing library was reimagined into a modern, futuristic and flexible learning space—bringing together the best of a library, reading café, digital learning hub and activity centre. The idea was to revive the reading habit among children and youth while making learning comfortable, engaging, relevant and fun.

But NALC was created for something bigger than books.

Its aim is to give every child and young person—regardless of background—a place to learn, prepare, explore their interests and build confidence. From children's books and fiction to academic resources and competitive-exam materials, NALC brings knowledge within reach of those who may not have a comfortable study space or access to expensive resources at home. Because learning doesn't stop at reading.

The NALC Philosophy

Read. Think. Explore. Prepare. Create. Grow.

Beyond Books. Beyond Boundaries.

Through quizzes, storytelling, public speaking, photography, painting, music, yoga, career guidance and other activity-based programmes, NALC encourages young minds to step beyond textbooks, discover their talents and develop their personalities. Because the goal isn't simply to produce better readers or better exam scores.

The goal is to help create confident, curious and capable young people who dare to dream bigger.

And that's why NALC Miao isn't just a library.

It's a place where curiosity gets a chance to become a future. 🚀
